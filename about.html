About.

Hi! I’m Cherry Ni, a CS student at UCLA. I’m passionate about web design and turning ideas into working products. I love being creative, whether it’s creating collages on Pinterest or designing posters on Canva.

I specialize in crafting accessible user interfaces using platforms such as WordPress and modern web tools. I thrive at the intersection of design and engineering, where great user experience meets clean, scalable code.

I’m currently learning software development and machine learning. I initially began my journey at UCLA studying chemical engineering, but after learning Python for the first time in a physics lab, I became fascinated with coding. Seeing abstract mathematical concepts transform into clean visual graphs scratched an itch in my brain, and I discovered a deep satisfaction in breaking down complex problems into logical, executable blocks of code.

I’m always looking for ways to grow as a developer and build tools that make the web more inclusive. Outside of coding, you can find me trying out new recipes, watching rom-coms, or exploring new cafes.

Want to say hi? My inbox is always open at cherryni@g.ucla.edu.
